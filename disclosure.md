## Affiliate links

Items on the [Deals]({{ '/deals/' | relative_url }}) page, and any item marked "Deal" with a "Get this deal" button, are sold by other stores, not by me. When you click one of those links and buy something, the store or an affiliate network may pay me a commission. You pay the same price either way.

**As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.**

Besides Amazon, I may use other affiliate programs and link services, for example:

- **Link routing services** such as Geniuslink, which send you to the right store for your country.
- **Automatic affiliate link services** such as Sovrn Commerce or Skimlinks. Their script can turn an ordinary store link on this site into an affiliate link when you click it.
- **Store affiliate programs** I join directly or through networks.

Affiliate links on this site are marked as sponsored in the page code. Whether a store pays a commission doesn't decide which deals I post. I post deals I think are worth it.

## Prices and availability change

Deal prices, coupons, and stock are set by the store and can change or end at any time, sometimes within minutes. The price you see here was right when I posted it. Always check the final price, shipping, and seller on the store's site before you buy. Questions about an order from another store go to that store.
