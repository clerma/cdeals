{%- comment -%} Prints the whole-number discount (e.g. 28) when the item has both prices and compare_at (original/list price) is higher than price. Prints nothing otherwise, and nothing for Amazon deals while Amazon prices are hidden. Usage: {% capture off %}{% include discount.html product=p %}{% endcapture %} {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _p = include.product -%} {%- capture _amz -%}{% include amazon-hidden.html product=_p %}{%- endcapture -%} {%- if _amz == "" and _p.compare_at and _p.price -%} {%- assign _was = _p.compare_at | times: 1.0 -%} {%- assign _now = _p.price | times: 1.0 -%} {%- if _was > _now -%} {%- assign _off = _now | divided_by: _was | times: 100 | minus: 100 | abs | round -%} {%- if _off >= 1 -%}{{ _off }}{%- endif -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endif -%}
