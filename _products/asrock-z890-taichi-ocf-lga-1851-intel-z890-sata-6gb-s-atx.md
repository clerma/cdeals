The ASRock Z890 Taichi OCF is an ATX motherboard for Intel Core Ultra (Series 2) processors on the LGA 1851 socket. I like it for builders who want to push their memory. Power delivery is a 22+1+2+1+1 phase design with 110A SPS for VCore. You also get two PCIe 5.0 x16 slots, two Thunderbolt 4 Type-C ports and Realtek ALC4082 7.1-channel audio with Nahimic.
