Ever since I was a kid, every trip to Target, Best Buy, Circuit City, RadioShack (and Service Merchandise, for you older folks) went the same way. I made a beeline for the electronics section. I could never buy anything, but I didn't need to. I read the spec cards, played the demo games, tapped on the keyboards, and dreamed up all the cool things I could build if I had that gear.

Not much has changed. I still window shop. The good news is that research, reviews and news about new tech are easier to find than ever. The surprising part is that good deals aren't. For most people, if it isn't at a local store or on Amazon, it might as well not exist. That never sat right with me. If I want something, I want to find it, and I want to find the best price on it.

Over the years, friends and family started asking me the same questions: "Seen any good deals lately?" and "Got any tablets or computers you'd be willing to part with?" Since I build websites for a living, it finally clicked. Why not make a place where anyone can see the deals I come across?

So I did it. I designed a logo, bought a domain, and started posting the best tech deals I find, along with some of my own well-cared-for gear that's up for sale (coming soon). cDeals is my one-stop shop for the tech finds I come across.

Take a look around. If you buy something through the site, some links may earn me a small commission at no extra cost to you. That helps keep the site running and the deals coming.

Thanks for stopping by. I hope you find the tech you've been looking for, at the best price out there.
